# Development screenshots

These are historical simulator captures used while building and auditing the interface. They include empty states and synthetic test fixtures; they do not establish real Apple Health authorization, real Watch data access or physical-device validation.

- `foundation-*`, `dashboard.png`, `health-connect.png`: foundation/dashboard and connection states.
- `nutrition-*`: local food editors, diary fixtures and larger-text layouts.
- `workout-*`: strength/cardio fixture flows.
- `progress-weight-fixture.png`: report chart with synthetic fixture data.
- `audit-small-*`: small-screen dashboard and accessibility-size food editor checks from the engineering audit.

See the dated milestone reports and `ENGINEERING_AUDIT.md` for the commands, limits and failed/inconclusive accessibility probes. A screenshot alone is not a passing test.
